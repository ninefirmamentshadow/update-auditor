for a mobile-first workflow, belonging to an independent operator.

protected-data boundary. See `README.md` for what the app reports and its
on-device gate, and `docs/V0_2_DESIGN.md` for the update-status roadmap.

Sovereign-Ops is the umbrella source of truth; this repository is the standalone
home of the app.
